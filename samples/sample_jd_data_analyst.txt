Job Title: Senior Data & Analytics Engineer
Company: MetroMetrics Data Labs
Location: San Jose, CA (Remote Friendly)

About the Role:
MetroMetrics is looking for an analytical, systems-minded Senior Data & Analytics Engineer to build robust data pipelines, optimize analytical query performance, and surface actionable business metrics from billions of raw telemetry events.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design and maintain end-to-end data ingestion pipelines handling over 500GB+ daily customer and operational data.
- Architect high-performance analytical queries, sharded schemas, and data aggregation pipelines in PostgreSQL and SQL.
- Develop data processing scripts and automated reporting workflows in Python using Pandas and SQLAlchemy.
- Partner with product analytics teams to construct dashboards, detect telemetry anomalies, and monitor real-time event streams with Kafka and Redis.
- Ensure strict data reliability, automated pipeline testing, and observability across all ETL workflows.

Requirements:
- 4+ years of data engineering, analytics engineering, or backend database development experience.
- Expert-level SQL and relational database query optimization (indexing, partitioning, latency tuning).
- Advanced Python data scripting skills (Pandas, automated ETL workflows).
- Experience working with high-volume streaming platforms (Apache Kafka) and caching layers (Redis).
- Proven ability to reduce query latency and extract quantitative insights from massive event datasets.

Nice to Have:
- Cloud certifications (AWS Solutions Architect, CKA).
- Published research or conference talks on database performance or distributed streaming architectures.
