Job Title: Senior Full-Stack Engineer
Company: CloudNexus Technologies
Location: San Francisco, CA (Hybrid)

About the Role:
We are seeking a talented Senior Full-Stack Engineer to architect and build high-throughput, low-latency web services and reactive user interfaces. You will join our core product engineering team driving our next-generation enterprise telemetry platform.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design, implement, and maintain resilient RESTful and event-driven microservices in Python (FastAPI).
- Build clean, responsive, and accessible user interfaces using React, TypeScript, and modern styling libraries.
- Optimize high-volume database queries, table indexing, and caching layers with PostgreSQL and Redis.
- Orchestrate containerized workloads in Docker and Kubernetes (EKS).
- Partner with product managers and mentors across the engineering organization to ship high-impact features.

Requirements:
- 4+ years of professional full-stack development experience.
- Expert proficiency in Python (FastAPI/Flask) and JavaScript/TypeScript (React).
- Strong experience with relational databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL) and ORMs (SQLAlchemy).
- Solid hands-on knowledge of Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD pipelines, and cloud environments (AWS).
- Demonstrated experience building reliable distributed systems serving millions of daily requests.

Nice to Have:
- Experience with Apache Kafka, GraphQL, and micro-frontend architectures.
- Contributions to open-source software libraries.
